The problem

Nobody can find it.
Not even you.

macOS lets you make the pointer 4× bigger and then stops. Where's My Mouse? goes to 15× for real, and to 30× with a pointer it draws itself. It shows up in your screen recordings too (tested in OBS).

Features

Small app. Huge pointer.

📏

Up to 30×

1–10× on the slider, 15× with the real pointer, and up to 30× when you need it seen from space.

🎨

Any fill, any outline

Hot pink with a black edge? Yellow on white? Pick a swatch or any color you like.

⚡

Live, no Settings

Changes apply instantly from the menu bar. No digging through Accessibility.

💾

Presets

Save "Recording", "Presenting", "Big Green". Switch in one click, update them anytime.

🤖

App rules

Pin a preset to an app: OBS goes blue, Loom goes orange. Follows the active app or the last one you opened.

🫥

Menu bar only

No Dock icon, launches at login, stays out of your way until you need it.

The menu

Everything in one little menu.

  • Presets with the apps pinned to them right on the row.
  • A size slider with marks from 1× to 10×, plus an extra-large mode to 30×.
  • Color swatches for fill and outline, or any custom color.
  • App rules that switch presets for you, then go back to Default.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. After the first launch attempt, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Where's My Mouse.app" once.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

Only for colors. macOS keeps pointer colors in its accessibility settings and only lets apps with Full Disk Access change them. Size works without it. The app reads nothing else.

Does the big pointer show up in screen recordings?

Yes. We tested it in OBS. Plain screenshots are the exception: macOS draws the pointer at normal size in still screenshots.

How does it go past System Settings' 4× limit?

The window server accepts sizes up to just under 16× through an undocumented call, so that's the real pointer up to 15×. From 16× to 30× the app hides the real pointer and draws its own arrow that follows your mouse. That one is always an arrow (no hand or text cursor).

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

It uses private macOS APIs and changes system accessibility settings, which App Store apps aren't allowed to do. So it's a direct download, like most pointer tools.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. Your pointer goes back to whatever you last set; pick Default first if you want the normal pointer.
