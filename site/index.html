"Can you see my mouse?"

Every screen share, ever. The default pointer is about 16 points tall, and it vanishes the moment your screen gets compressed, projected or watched on a phone.

Nobody can find it.
Not even you.

Everyone says “just shake it.” And sure, shake your mouse and the pointer briefly balloons so you can spot it. Then it shrinks right back, and you're hunting again.

macOS's shake tops out at 4×. Where's My Mouse? starts there.

What if you never had to shake it to find it? Where's My Mouse? keeps your pointer big all the time: 15× for real, or 30× with a pointer it draws itself, in a color nobody can miss. It shows up in your screen recordings too (tested in OBS).

For screen recording

The problem: your viewers watch on phones. After compression, a 16-point pointer is a smudge, and the comments fill up with “where did you click?”

The fix: pin a big blue “Recording” preset to OBS or Loom. Open the app and the pointer grows on its own; quit it and you're back to normal.

For education & tutorials

The problem: step-by-step tutorials live or die on “click here.” When viewers can't follow the pointer, they pause, rewind, and give up.

The fix: a bright, oversized pointer is your built-in highlighter. Every click is obvious, with no zoom effects or editing tricks afterward.

For teachers

The problem: from the back row, a projected pointer disappears, and students on a shared screen at home see even less.

The fix: one click to a huge, high-contrast pointer for class, one click back to Default after. It helps low-vision students too.

For presentations

The problem: you say “right over here” on a conference screen and three hundred people look everywhere except where you're pointing.

The fix: give Keynote or your browser a big red “Presenting” preset. It switches on automatically whenever that app is in front.

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub.

Why does macOS say it can't verify the app?

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet. After the first launch attempt, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Where's My Mouse.app" once.

Why does it need Full Disk Access?

Only for colors. macOS keeps pointer colors in its accessibility settings and only lets apps with Full Disk Access change them. Size works without it. The app reads nothing else.

Does the big pointer show up in screen recordings?

Yes. We tested it in OBS. Plain screenshots are the exception: macOS draws the pointer at normal size in still screenshots.

How does it go past System Settings' 4× limit?

The window server accepts sizes up to just under 16× through an undocumented call, so that's the real pointer up to 15×. From 16× to 30× the app hides the real pointer and draws its own arrow that follows your mouse. That one is always an arrow (no hand or text cursor).

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

It uses private macOS APIs and changes system accessibility settings, which App Store apps aren't allowed to do. So it's a direct download, like most pointer tools.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. Your pointer goes back to whatever you last set; pick Default first if you want the normal pointer.
