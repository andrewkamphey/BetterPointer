Cursor Recipes

32 ready-made pointer looks. Open Where's My Mouse? in the menu bar, set the size, pick the fill and outline, and save it as a preset. Click any size to try it bigger or smaller.

Colors need macOS 12 or later. Sizes above 15× need Extra large turned on.
